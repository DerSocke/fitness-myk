How a workout goes

At the gym all you need is your watch, or your phone if you don't have a watch. Big buttons and vibration instead of sound, so it works even with sweaty fingers.

Pick a plan

Start right on the phone, or on the watch: the plans come from the phone, after that it works without a connection.

Check off a set

Weight and reps are already filled in. One tap is enough, and you can change them too.

Rest timer runs

The timer starts by itself and vibrates shortly before and at the end of the rest.

On through the plan

After the last set comes the next exercise. At the end, the workout is saved.

Features

You can train on the watch or on the phone, and plans, history and statistics live on the phone. With a watch, both sync automatically as soon as they are connected.

On the watch

  • Weight and reps for every set come straight from your plan
  • One tap checks off the set, with big buttons
  • Change the weight in 2.5 kg steps or set it to the exact 0.25 kg with a scroll wheel
  • The rest timer keeps running with the display off, with a warning 10 seconds before the end and a strong vibration
  • During the rest you see the next exercise with set and weight
  • Per plan: continue automatically, or let the rest keep counting until you tap “Next”
  • Add or skip single sets during a workout
  • Warm-up sets are marked as such on the watch
  • A running workout never ends by accident: when you start another plan, you choose between continuing and starting over
  • Works at the gym without phone and without internet

On the phone

  • Train without any watch: check off sets on the phone, with rest timer, vibration and countdown in the notification
  • Optional voice announcements during a workout on the phone: end of rest, next exercise, exercise note and end of workout
  • Create exercises and workout plans and set their order
  • Default sets, weight, reps and rest per exercise, with an overview of the plans it is used in
  • Sets, weight, reps and rest, if you like for each set separately (e.g. pyramid training), single sets marked as warm-up
  • Optionally, the plan adopts the weights you actually lifted after the workout
  • Add exercises from other plans to a running workout
  • Notes on plans and exercises, photos and videos for the correct technique
  • Statistics: workouts, volume, sets and duration per week or month, plus your streak of training weeks
  • Per exercise: progress of weight, estimated 1RM and volume, your records and a note when you set a new one
  • Warm-up sets do not count towards volume and records
  • History of all workouts with date and time; correct or delete single sets later, also whole exercises or workouts
  • Continue a workout from the history, also one from the watch, for example when its battery is empty
  • Sync with the watch at the push of a button, showing when it last synced
  • Backup to a file, share plans with friends, history as CSV for Excel

Good to know

Your data stays with you

No ads, no account, no sign-in. Your workout data is stored only on your watch and your phone. Details in the privacy policy.

Requirements

  • Android phone with Android 10 or later
  • Optional: smartwatch with Wear OS 3 or later, e.g. Samsung Galaxy Watch4 and newer
  • With a watch: it needs the connection to the phone once, after that you train without it
  • Weights in kilograms
  • App in English, German or French, following the language of your device
