Help and FAQ

Fitness Myk for Android phones and Wear OS watches

Getting started

How do I start?

On the phone, add your exercises with + in the “Exercises” tab. In the “Plans” tab, create a workout plan with + and add the exercises in the order you want. To train, tap “Start workout” in the plan on the phone, or choose the plan on the watch.

Do I need a watch?

No. A workout runs the same way on the phone, with rest timer, vibration and a countdown in the notification. With a Wear OS watch, the phone can stay in the locker.

How do I get the app onto the watch?

Open the Play Store on the watch and search for “Fitness Myk”. One purchase covers phone and watch. Watch and phone need to be paired, for example with the Galaxy Wearable app.

Why are there no plans on the watch?

Plans and exercises are created on the phone only. The watch receives them when syncing, so it needs the connection to the phone once. Open the app on both devices and tap the sync button. After that, you can train on the watch without the phone.

Training

How does the rest timer work?

As soon as you tick off a set, the rest starts. For rests longer than 15 seconds, the device gives a short buzz 10 seconds before the end and a strong one at the end. You set the rest for each exercise in the plan on the phone.

The rest does not show on the watch face.

Allow notifications for Fitness Myk on the watch (watch settings → Apps → Fitness Myk → Notifications). Without this permission the rest timer only runs inside the app.

The next set does not start by itself after the rest.

This is set per plan: “Continue automatically after rest”. When it is off, the timer keeps counting below zero (−0:05, −0:10 …) until you tap “Next”.

Can I change weight and reps during a workout?

Yes. On the watch, +/− changes the weight in steps of 2.5 kg, and tapping the value opens scroll wheels for exact values down to 0.25 kg. If a value you tick off differs from the target, it becomes the target for the following sets of that exercise in this workout. The plan stays as it is, unless you switch on “Adopt weights from the workout” for that plan entry.

Can I set different values for each set, for example for pyramids?

Yes. Switch on “Set values for each set” in the plan entry. Each set then has its own row with weight, reps and rest. Tapping the set number marks a warm-up set. Warm-up sets do not count in the statistics.

I forgot to finish the workout.

A workout without activity ends by itself after 8 hours, at the time of the last set. If you start another plan before that, the app asks whether to continue or to start a new one.

The watch battery died in the middle of a workout.

The phone shows the running workout in the plan list (“Running on the watch”). Tap “Continue on the phone” to go on there. A finished workout can be continued from the history for up to 8 hours after its last set.

How do I switch on voice announcements?

On the phone, open the menu at the top right → “Voice announcements”. There you choose what is announced and which voice is used. Announcements are only available when training on the phone; the watch uses vibration.

History, statistics and data

How do I correct or delete a set?

Open the workout in the “History” tab and tap the set. There you change the values or delete the set. The bin icons delete all sets of one exercise or the whole workout. Changes are passed on to the watch when syncing.

A workout from the watch is missing on the phone.

Watch and phone sync as soon as they are connected. Tap the sync button; the phone then shows when data last arrived. Keep the watch and phone apps on the same version by updating both.

What does “1RM (est.)” mean?

It estimates how much you could lift for a single rep, calculated with the Epley formula: weight × (1 + reps / 30). Above 10 reps the estimate becomes less reliable.

Where is my data, and how do I back it up?

Only on your watch and your phone. There is no account and no server. Use the menu at the top right → “Create backup” to save all data to a file. On a new phone, restore it with “Import backup or plan”. Photos and videos of exercises are not part of the backup.

Can I analyse my workouts in Excel?

Yes, with the menu → “Export history as CSV”. The file opens directly in Excel.

How do I pass a plan on to friends?

In the plan, tap “Share plan” at the top and send the file. Whoever opens it with “Import backup or plan” gets a copy of the plan.
