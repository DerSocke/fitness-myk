Privacy policy

for the app “Fitness Myk” for Android and Wear OS and for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch · as of 11/2026

2. Collection and processing of data

The app “Fitness Myk” stores your entries and settings exclusively locally on your own devices (smartphone and watch). I as the developer receive no data, and nothing is transmitted to me or to third parties. The app contains no advertising or analytics services. What Apple provides to me for iPhone and Apple Watch with your consent or during a test via TestFlight is described below.

Android and Wear OS

Between smartphone and watch, the app syncs your data via Google Play services. This sync takes place only between your own paired devices. Google’s privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Android may back up the app’s data as part of the Google backup of your Google account and restore it on a new device. You control this yourself in the Android settings. I have no access to it.

iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

The app for iPhone and iPad does not connect to the internet. Between iPhone and Apple Watch, it syncs your data via Apple’s system feature for paired devices (WatchConnectivity), only between your iPhone and your own watch.

iOS may include the app’s data, including the pictures and videos for your exercises, in the backup of your device (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer). You control this yourself in your iPhone’s settings. Apple’s privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy. I have no access to it.

So that the rest timer keeps running when your arm is lowered, the app on the Apple Watch keeps a watchOS background session open during a workout. It does not use Apple Health (HealthKit) for this and reads no health or sensor data such as heart rate, motion or location.

If you have agreed in the settings of your iPhone or Apple Watch to share analytics with app developers, Apple provides me with crash reports and aggregated usage statistics in a form that does not personally identify you. You can turn this off at any time under “Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements”.

Testing via TestFlight

If you use a test version via Apple’s TestFlight app, I receive from Apple the email address you were invited with, information on when you installed and used the test version, crash reports, and feedback and screenshots you send yourself via TestFlight. I use this information only to test and improve the app. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (your participation in the test). Apple’s privacy policy also applies to TestFlight.

3. Photos and videos, permissions

On the smartphone you can add pictures and videos to exercises. They are selected via the system photo picker; the app only accesses the files you select yourself and gets no access to the rest of your photo library. These media stay on your smartphone and are transferred neither to the watch nor to me or third parties. When you open a picture or video, the app can provide it to another app on your device (e.g. gallery or video player) for viewing only.

For the rest timer, the app uses vibration and a notification showing the remaining rest time on watch and smartphone. On iPhone and Apple Watch, the app asks for permission to send notifications; without it, the app keeps working, just without notifications. During a rest, the iPhone shows a Live Activity on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island with the remaining time, the plan, the next set and its weight. This information is visible even while the iPhone is locked; Live Activities can be turned off in the app’s settings. Voice announcements are produced by your device’s speech output without an internet connection. No data is transmitted in the process. Beyond that, the app requests no special permissions, for example for camera, microphone, location, contacts or health data.

4. Purchase via Google Play and the App Store

The app is distributed via Google Play and via Apple’s App Store. Purchase and payment are handled exclusively by Google or Apple respectively. I receive no payment data. You can find information on this in the privacy policies of Google at policies.google.com/privacy and of Apple at apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. Your rights

Apart from the information from a test via TestFlight (section 2), I process no personal data. Under the GDPR you generally have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You can also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority.

6. Changes

I will adapt this policy when the app or the legal situation changes. You will always find the current version on this page.

7. Contact

If you have questions about data protection, you can reach me at support (at) kansok.com.
