Privacy policy

2. Collection and processing of data

The app “Fitness Myk” stores your entries and settings exclusively locally on your own devices (smartphone and watch). I as the developer receive no data, and nothing is transmitted to me or to third parties. The app contains no advertising or analytics services.

Between smartphone and watch, the app syncs your data via Google Play services. This sync takes place only between your own paired devices. Google’s privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Android may back up the app’s data as part of the Google backup of your Google account and restore it on a new device. You control this yourself in the Android settings. I have no access to it.

3. Photos and videos, permissions

On the smartphone you can add pictures and videos to exercises. They are selected via the system photo picker; the app only accesses the files you select yourself. These media stay on your smartphone and are transferred neither to the watch nor to me or third parties. When you open a picture or video, the app can provide it to another app on your device (e.g. gallery or video player) for viewing only.

For the rest timer, the app uses vibration and a notification showing the remaining rest time on watch and smartphone. No data is transmitted in the process. Beyond that, the app requests no special permissions, for example for camera, microphone, location or contacts.

4. Purchase via Google Play

The app is distributed via Google Play. Purchase and payment are handled exclusively by Google. I receive no payment data. You can find information on this in Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Your rights

Since no personal data is processed, I cannot provide any information about it. Under the GDPR you generally have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You can also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority.

6. Changes

I will adapt this policy when the app or the legal situation changes. You will always find the current version on this page.

7. Contact

If you have questions about data protection, you can reach me at support (at) kansok.com.
